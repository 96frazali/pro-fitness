Pro Fitness Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Pro Fitness is a personal fitness-planning app. This policy applies to the version distributed with package name com.repwise.fitness.

Data stored on your device

Pro Fitness stores the information you enter on your device to provide your plan and history. This can include your name, email address, age, sex, height, weight and target weight, training preferences and limitations, workout history, food entries, body measurements, reminder settings, readiness check-ins, and progress photos you choose to add.

How data is used

The app uses this information on your device to calculate nutrition targets, tailor training plans, show workout and progress history, and schedule reminders that you enable. This release has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or cloud-sync service.

Sharing and collection

Pro Fitness does not transmit, sell, rent, or share your fitness information or photos with us or third parties. Information remains on your device unless you choose Export my data. Tutorial buttons may open YouTube searches in your browser; those pages are governed by YouTube's own terms and privacy policy.

Your controls

You can export your local information or use Delete all local data in Profile. Deleting local data removes the Pro Fitness profile and tracking data stored by the app on that device. You can also remove individual progress photos from Profile.

Notifications and Health Connect

Workout reminders are optional and scheduled only after you enable them. Pro Fitness can check whether Health Connect is available on an Android device, but this release does not read or write Health Connect records.

Children and security

Pro Fitness is not designed for children. The app uses device-local storage and disables Android device backup for its app data. No internet service is provided by this release for collecting fitness data.
