# Pro Fitness privacy policy

**Effective date:** September 10, 2026

## Overview

Pro Fitness is a personal fitness-planning app. This policy describes the data handled by the version of Pro Fitness distributed with package name `com.repwise.fitness`.

## Data stored on your device

Pro Fitness stores the information you enter in the app on your device so it can provide your plan and history. This may include your name, email address, age, sex, height, weight and target weight, training preferences and limitations, workout history, food entries, body measurements, reminder settings, readiness check-ins, and progress photos you choose to add.

## How data is used

Pro Fitness uses this information on your device to calculate nutrition targets, tailor training plans, show workout and progress history, and schedule reminders that you enable. The release covered by this policy does not include an account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or cloud-sync service.

## Sharing and collection

Pro Fitness does not transmit, sell, rent, or share your fitness information or photos with us or third parties in this release. Information remains on your device unless you choose **Export my data**. Tutorial buttons may open YouTube searches in your browser; those pages are governed by YouTube's own terms and privacy policy.

## Controls

You can export your local information or use **Delete all local data** in Profile. Deleting local data removes the profile and Pro Fitness data stored by the app on that device. You can also remove individual progress photos from Profile.

## Notifications and Health Connect

Workout reminders are optional and are scheduled only after you enable them. Pro Fitness can check whether Health Connect is available on an Android device, but this release does not read or write Health Connect records.

## Children

Pro Fitness is not designed for children. Set the appropriate age group in Play Console before publishing and do not market the app to children unless the app and policy are updated for the applicable requirements.

## Security

Pro Fitness uses device-local storage for the data described above and disables Android device-backup for its app data. No internet service is provided by this release for collecting fitness data.
